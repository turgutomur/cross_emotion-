\section{Full Pairwise Bootstrap Results}
\label{app:bootstrap}

Table~\ref{tab:fullbootstrap} reports every pairwise method comparison
for which a paired bootstrap test is defined, organized by protocol
and target. A test is defined only when both methods in the pair have
three training seeds: predictions are concatenated across the three
seeds and the same 1000 resampling indices (seed~42) are applied to
both methods within each resample. Pairs in which either method has a
single seed --- DANN on all four protocols, and DANN+Focal and
CDAN+Focal on GoEmotions LODO, ISEAR LODO, and Mixed --- are not
testable and are omitted; this accounts for the 41 method pairs not
listed below. The 19 testable comparisons are the complete set
summarized in Table~\ref{tab:bootstrap} of the main text.
