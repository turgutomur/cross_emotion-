% Ethics statement — short, focused on dataset use and emotion-classifier risks.

Emotion classification raises two ethical concerns that we acknowledge.

First, the three datasets we use (GoEmotions, ISEAR, WASSA-21) were
collected under different consent regimes and represent different
populations. GoEmotions sources Reddit comments that, while publicly
posted, were not collected with explicit ML-research consent. ISEAR
participants were recruited for psychological research with consent
appropriate to that purpose. WASSA-21 essays were collected for a
shared task with task-specific consent. We use all three datasets
within the terms of their public releases and do not deanonymize
content.

Second, emotion classifiers deployed cross-corpus risk surfacing
distribution-shift errors as predictions, with the appearance of
calibrated confidence. Our paper's central finding — that no method we
tested closes the validation-to-test gap on two of three LODO targets
— is itself a caution against deploying single-corpus-trained emotion
classifiers to held-out populations or genres. We report the gap
explicitly (Section~\ref{sec:results:gap}) to give practitioners a
calibrated estimate of cross-dataset error.

We do not release new emotion data with this submission, only model
configurations and analysis code. The released artifacts pose no
additional privacy risk beyond what is already in the source corpora.
