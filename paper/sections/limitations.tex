\paragraph{Single-seed cells for some adversarial-focal combinations.}
Three cells in our main results table (Table~\ref{tab:main}) are
reported at a single seed (DANN+Focal and CDAN+Focal on GoEmotions,
ISEAR, and Mixed; see $^\dagger$ annotations). The seed-42 trends on
these cells were strongly negative (more than $1.5\sigma$ below CE
source-only), and we reallocated compute to confirming the WASSA-21
positive trend at three seeds and running the 5-seed DeBERTa-large
robustness validation. We do not include single-seed cells in
significance tests and we transparently mark them as such.

\paragraph{WASSA-21 target test set size.}
The WASSA-21 LODO target test split contains 164 examples across
six Ekman classes (median 17 per class). This is the released
empathy-track essay distribution. While it is sufficient for our
main bootstrap test ($p = \focalwassap$ at three seeds with
DeBERTa-base), it limits the resolution of comparisons at the
DeBERTa-large scale, where higher model capacity inflates seed
variance disproportionately.

\paragraph{WASSA-21 validation/test split fallback.}
The official WASSA-21 release distributes train, validation, and test
files; only the training file is labeled in the public release. Our
loader falls back to a stratified $80/10/10$ split of the labeled
training file with random seed 42. The same split is applied to all
six methods, so within-paper comparisons are internally consistent,
but results are not directly comparable to other papers that may
have evaluated on the original WASSA-21 evaluation server with held-out
labels.

\paragraph{Pre-registered Ekman-6 projection trade-offs.}
We excluded \texttt{shame} and \texttt{guilt} from the ISEAR-to-Ekman
projection because the shame-to-sadness mapping is contested in the
literature \citep{bostan2018analysis}. As a consequence, ISEAR
contributes five Ekman classes; \emph{surprise} is absent from the
dataset entirely. This is a known limitation of any work using ISEAR
under Ekman-6 unification. Macro-F1 on ISEAR is computed over the
classes present in the target evaluation split, which is consistent
across all six methods we compare.

\paragraph{Scope of the DeBERTa-large validation.}
We run DeBERTa-v3-large only on the single strongest cell (CE + Focal
$\times$ WASSA-21 LODO) per the pre-registered "best-two final"
5-seed protocol. We do not report large-model results for other
method-protocol cells due to compute budget. Whether the negative
results on adversarial methods replicate at large scale is therefore
unknown; we conjecture they do, based on the source-overfitting
diagnosis (Section~\ref{sec:discussion:scale}), but do not have
empirical evidence.

\paragraph{Single-corpus evaluation per LODO fold.}
The LODO protocol holds out exactly one target dataset per fold. We do
not evaluate on out-of-domain corpora beyond the three datasets we
unify (e.g.\ EmoBank, GoodNewsEveryone). Whether the focal-loss
advantage extends to fourth-domain transfer or to non-Ekman emotion
schemata is an open question.

\paragraph{Backbone choice.}
We use DeBERTa-v3 throughout. Whether the findings replicate with
RoBERTa, Llama-2 fine-tuned classifiers, or instruction-tuned models is
left to future work.
